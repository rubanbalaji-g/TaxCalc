📖 Complete Deductions Guide

Every deduction available under Old & New Tax Regime for FY 2025-26

🏦

Section 80C / 80CCC / 80CCD(1)

Combined Limit: ₹1,50,000
  • EPF – Employee contribution to Provident Fund
  • PPF – Public Provident Fund deposits (min 15 yr)
  • ELSS – Equity Linked Savings Scheme (3-yr lock)
  • LIC / Term Insurance – Premium paid for life insurance
  • NSC – National Savings Certificate (5 yr)
  • 5-Yr Tax FD – Scheduled bank FDs
  • Home Loan Principal – Repayment of housing loan
  • SSY – Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (girl child)
  • SCSS – Senior Citizens Savings Scheme
  • Tuition Fees – Up to 2 children's school/college fees
  • 80CCC – Pension fund contributions (LIC / other)
  • 80CCD(1) – Employee NPS contribution (10% of basic)
🏛️

Section 80CCD(1B) – NPS Extra

Limit: ₹50,000 (over & above 80C)
  • Additional contribution to NPS Tier-I account
  • Reduces taxable income by up to ₹50,000 extra
  • Total NPS benefit: up to ₹2,00,000 with 80C
  • Effective tax saving up to ₹15,600 (30% slab)
🏥

Section 80D – Health Insurance

Up to ₹1,00,000 combined
  • Self & Family – ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if you are Senior Citizen)
  • Parents – ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if parents are Senior Citizens)
  • Preventive Check-up – ₹5,000 within above limits
  • Max deduction: ₹1,00,000 if both self & parents are senior citizens
♿

80DD / 80DDB / 80U – Disability

Fixed / as incurred
  • 80DD – Disabled dependent: ₹75,000 / ₹1,25,000 (≥80%)
  • 80DDB – Specified disease: ₹40,000 (₹1,00,000 for seniors)
  • 80U – Self disability: ₹75,000 / ₹1,25,000 (≥80%)
🏠

Home Loan Deductions

Up to ₹3,50,000+ per year
  • Sec 24(b) – Interest on self-occupied home loan: ₹2,00,000
  • Sec 24(b) – Interest on let-out property: Actual (no limit)
  • 80C – Principal repayment: Within ₹1,50,000 cap
  • 80EE – First-home extra interest: ₹50,000
  • 80EEA – Affordable housing extra: ₹1,50,000
📚

Education & Donations

Unlimited (80E) / 10% GTI (80G)
  • 80E – Education loan interest (higher education): No limit, 8 yrs
  • 80G (100%) – PM CARES, PM National Relief Fund, etc.
  • 80G (50%) – Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Fund, etc.
  • 80GGA – Scientific/rural research donations: 100%
  • 80GGC – Political party donations: 100%
🏡

HRA & LTA Exemptions

Sec 10(13A) & Sec 10(5)
  • HRA Exempt = Least of: Actual HRA | Rent–10%Basic | 50%/40% of Basic
  • LTA – Actual travel cost for 2 journeys in 4-yr block (domestic)
  • 80GG – If no HRA: Least of rent–10% income / ₹5000/mo / 25% income
📋

Standard & Professional Tax

Sec 16
  • Standard Deduction [Sec 16(ia)] – ₹50,000 (Flat, no proof needed)
  • Professional Tax [Sec 16(iii)] – Actual paid, max ₹2,500
  • Entertainment Allowance [Sec 16(ii)] – Government employees only
🏧

80TTA / 80TTB – Interest Income

₹10,000 / ₹50,000
  • 80TTA – Savings bank interest: Up to ₹10,000 (non-senior)
  • 80TTB – All interest (savings + FD + PO): Up to ₹50,000 (senior citizens only)
  • Cannot claim both 80TTA and 80TTB
⚡

80EEB – Electric Vehicle Loan

Up to ₹1,50,000
  • Interest on loan taken to purchase EV
  • Loan must be sanctioned Apr 2019 – Mar 2023
  • Applicable for both 2-wheelers and 4-wheelers
✨

New Regime – Simplified Structure

The New Tax Regime offers lower slab rates but most deductions & exemptions are NOT available. Only the following are allowed:

✅

Allowed Deductions / Exemptions

  • ✅ Standard Deduction – ₹75,000 (salaried/pensioners)
  • ✅ 80CCD(2) – Employer NPS contribution (10% of basic)
  • ✅ Employer PF – Exempt up to ₹7.5 lakh per year (employer contributions)
  • ✅ Gratuity – Exempt up to ₹20 lakh
  • ✅ Leave Encashment – Exempt at retirement (Govt. employees: fully)
  • ✅ Agniveer Corpus Fund – 100% exempt
  • ✅ Perquisites – Free food (up to ₹50/meal), official travel, etc.
  • ✅ Sec 54 to 54F – Capital gain exemptions on reinvestment
  • ✅ Disability Perquisite – Transport, uniform allowances for disabled
❌

NOT Available in New Regime

  • ❌ Section 80C (EPF, PPF, ELSS, LIC, NSC etc.)
  • ❌ Section 80D (Health Insurance)
  • ❌ Section 80CCD(1B) (Additional NPS)
  • ❌ HRA Exemption [Sec 10(13A)]
  • ❌ LTA Exemption [Sec 10(5)]
  • ❌ Section 24(b) – Home Loan Interest
  • ❌ Section 80E – Education Loan
  • ❌ Section 80G – Donations
  • ❌ Section 80TTA / 80TTB – Savings Interest
  • ❌ Section 80DD / 80DDB / 80U
  • ❌ Professional Tax Deduction
  • ❌ Section 80EE / 80EEA / 80EEB
  • ❌ Chapter VI-A deductions (except 80CCD(2))
